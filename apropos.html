About

A passion for braiding and quality at the service of your look.

My story

Hello, my name is Rose, owner of African Hair Braiding by Alvine, located in San Antonio, Texas.

I specialize in African hair braiding and have been braiding with passion since Africa. I have over 10 years of experience in the hair industry. I previously owned a hair salon which I had to close during Covid-19. Now, I welcome you to my private home salon, a more comfortable and relaxing space to better serve you.

My promise

If you’re looking for neat, precise, high-quality braids, I’m the braider for you. My priority is to make you happy.

The salon

A comfortable, private home salon in San Antonio where you can relax while getting the braids you want.
